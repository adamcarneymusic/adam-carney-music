About Adam

A life in music.

Adam Carney is an Austin-based guitarist, composer, bandleader, and educator. Equally at home in classical music, Brazilian choro, tango, and jazz, he brings a broad musical perspective to both solo performances and collaborative projects.

He performs on seven-string classical guitar and a custom-built fourteen-string harp guitar — an instrument whose sound combines the character of classical guitar with the resonance of a concert harp.

Adam works with a versatile community of professional musicians, creating ensembles ranging from solo guitar to larger groups for concerts, cultural events, and private performances throughout Austin and Central Texas.

Selected recordings & credits

Elizabeth — Solo Harp Guitar (2009)

Libertango — Austin Piazzolla Quintet (2011)

Hora Once — Nuevo Tango / Latin Folk (2016)

His music has appeared in film, television, video games, documentaries, and international commercials.
