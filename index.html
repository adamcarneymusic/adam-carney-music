Violet Crown Affair

Violet Crown Affair is an intimate chamber duo featuring custom 14-string harp guitar and cello. Their repertoire includes original compositions and their own arrangements of classical, contemporary, and film music, with an emphasis on the unique possibilities of this unusual pairing.

Caros Amigos

Founded in 2021, Caros Amigos features flute, saxophone, 7-string guitar and percussion. The group specializes in Brazilian music, especially choro, alongside standards and original compositions.

Musicians include Julie Koidin (flute), Scotty McIntosh (saxophone), Adam Carney (7-string guitar) and Sergio Santos (percussion).

Austin Choro Club

A free community for local musicians and music lovers interested in the history and tradition of Brazilian choro. The club brings people together for a regular roda—an informal musical circle for playing, improvising, listening and sharing music. Musicians of all instruments and skill levels, as well as listeners, are welcome.

The club's newsletter shares tunes to learn, Brazilian music history, local events, sheet music and learning resources.

Biography

Adam Carney is an Austin-based guitarist, composer, bandleader and guitar instructor.

He performs regularly in Austin and throughout Central Texas on 7-string classical guitar and custom 14-string harp guitar, both as a soloist and with ensembles including Caros Amigos, Hora Once and The Violet Crown Affair. His work spans Brazilian choro, Argentine tango, classical music, jazz and original compositions.

His recording work includes the 2007 swing jazz album Gypsy Moon; his first solo harp guitar album, Elizabeth (2009); the award-winning Nuevo Tango album Libertango with the Austin Piazzolla Quintet (2011); and the 2016 Nuevo Tango and Latin Folk album Hora Once. He is currently working on his fifth studio album with Austin-based choro group Caros Amigos.

His music has been featured in films, television shows, video games, documentaries, international television commercials and the FX series Archer.

Adam is also a classical guitar instructor and the founder and director of Capital City Guitar Camp in Austin, which began in 2014.

About the Harp Guitar

The harp guitar has a standard guitar neck as well as additional sub-bass strings. The bass strings are played with the thumb, adding deep bass notes and drones while melodies and rhythms are played on the guitar neck. Adam performs on two rare 14-string custom harp guitars, designed by him and built by master luthiers Sean Wooley in France and Woodley White in Hawaii.
